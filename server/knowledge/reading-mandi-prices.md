# How to read mandi prices

## Units
Mandi prices are quoted in Rupees per quintal. One quintal is 100 kg. To get a rough price per kg, divide the quintal price by 100. For example, Rs 2,500 per quintal is about Rs 25 per kg.

## Min, max and modal price
The minimum price is the lowest rate paid that day for the commodity at that market. The maximum price is the highest rate paid. The modal price is the rate at which most of the trade happened, so it is the best single number for "what is the going rate". The max price is usually for the best quality lots and is not what an average lot will fetch.

## Why prices differ between markets
Prices differ because of local supply (arrivals), local demand, quality and variety, and transport cost. A market with a higher price is not always better once you subtract the cost and time of transporting the produce there, plus any weight loss on the way.

## What affects a day's price
Large arrivals usually push prices down and small arrivals push them up. Quality, moisture, size and variety all change the rate. Festival demand, weather and what neighbouring states are supplying also move prices.
